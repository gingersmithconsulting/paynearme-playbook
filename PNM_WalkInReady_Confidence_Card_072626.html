🔍 5 Discovery Questions to Open Every Deal
1
"Walk me through exactly what a member experiences when they want to make a loan payment today."
2
"What payment channels do your members ask for that you don't currently offer?"
3
"How much of your delinquency is attributed to members who genuinely can't pay vs. members who just didn't get around to it?"
4
"Is your current self-serve option actually self-serve — or is it just a detour back to your call center?"
5
"What percentage of your loan payments come through your call center — do you have a sense of that number?"
Flip the risk: Staying with a legacy payment system is riskier than changing. Delinquency compounds, younger members defect, and agent-assisted costs keep climbing. Help them see the cost of inaction before they ask about the cost of moving. If they've answered honestly, the cost of staying is already clear.
