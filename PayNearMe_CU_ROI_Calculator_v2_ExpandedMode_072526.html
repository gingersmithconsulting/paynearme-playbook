Model assumptions: Call deflection savings are calculated as: monthly call volume × deflection rate × member adoption rate × cost per call. Delinquency recovery is calculated as: total loan portfolio × current delinquency rate × delinquency reduction % × member adoption rate (applied as annual recovered balance). Platform investment is adjustable above — confirm actual pricing with your PayNearMe sales leader before presenting. Expanded Mode adds Modern Payment Processing (downtime savings = downtime minutes recovered × payments/hour × cost per delayed payment; reconciliation savings = hours/day × hourly employee cost × 260 working days × % reduction) and Wider Payment Options (in-person savings = in-person payments/month × cost per in-person payment × % reduction; disbursement savings = paper disbursements/month × % switching to electronic × net cost per switch) and Exception Management (exception handling savings = exceptions avoided × minutes to resolve × hourly employee cost; chargeback/return savings = count avoided × average payment amount; fraud/write-off savings = monthly losses × % reduction; recency savings = loans moved to recent × average payment amount — this last one uses a deliberately simpler model than PayNearMe's own and should be validated against a closed deal before quoting) and Automation & Self-Service (inquiry-call savings = inquiry calls deflected × cost per inquiry call, scaled by the same Member Adoption Rate used above — this is separate call volume from the Agent-Assisted Loan Payments field, which already covers payment/disbursement calls, so the two do not double-count) and Payment Abandonment & Recovery — sourced directly from PayNearMe's own 2026 published research, not LeveragePoint's driver set (in-the-moment recovery savings = failed payments recovered × average payment amount, using PayNearMe's published 9% recovery rate; re-engagement nudge savings = abandoned payments completed after nudge × average payment amount, using PayNearMe's published 7% completion rate). All figures are estimates for illustrative and discussion purposes only. Actual results will vary based on institution size, member behavior, implementation approach, and market conditions. This model is intended to support discovery conversations — not replace a formal business case. Validate all inputs with actual institution data before presenting.
